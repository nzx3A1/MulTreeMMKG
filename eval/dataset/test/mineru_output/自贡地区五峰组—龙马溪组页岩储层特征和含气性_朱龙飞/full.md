# Characteristics and gas bearing properties of gas shale reservoirs in Wufeng-Longmaxi formations, Zigong region

Zhu Longfei1 Xu Zhuang1 Liu Chenlu1 Xu Qinghai2 Zeng Fancheng3 Shang Pengxiang （ . School of Earth and Planetary Sciences, East China University of Technology, Nanchang . School of Earth Sciences, Yangtze University Wuhan . Chuannan Natural Gas Development Division of Jilin Oilfield Branch of China National Petroleum Corporation Zigong, Sichuan ）

Abstract Shale exploration in multiple regions of the Sichuan Basin has achieved breakthroughs, with stable production growth in shale gas extraction from the Luzhou and Changning areas. The Zigong region, adiacent to the Luzhou block, has shown promising exploration potential for the Wufeng-Longmaxi formations shale based on preliminary studies. However, insufficient research on shale reservoir characteristics and gas⁃bearing properties in this area has severely hindered further shale gas exploration. This study investigates the reservoir characteristics and gas-bearing properties of the Wufeng-Longmaxi formations shale in the Zigong area through experimenta analyses, including X⁃ray diffraction, field emission scanning electron microscopy, overburden pressure porosity tests, and organic carbon analysis. A comparative analysis with the neighboring Luzhou block was also conducted. The results indicate that the Wufeng-Longmaxi formations shale in Zigong exhibits high organic matter abundance, with an average total organic carbon （TOC） content of 2.80%, and is in an overmature stage, characterized by an average vitrinite reflectance （Ro） of 2.49%. The reservoir exhibits relatively high porosity （average 4.81%）, with diverse pore types such as inorganic mineral pores and organic matter pores. The shale is characterized by high quartz content, low clay content, and high brittle mineral content, with average brittle index values （brittleness index I and Ⅱ ) of 55.71% and 68.77%, respectively, indicating favorable brittleness. The shale reservoir pressure coefficient exceeds 2.0, and the gas content ranges from 3.28 m3/t to 5.98 m3/t, demonstrating favorable gas⁃bearing capacity. Compared to the Luzhou area, the Zigong region exhibits higher porosity, thermal maturity, and brittleness indices, while the TOC and gas content are slightly lower. Both regions share similar pore types. Overall, the Wufeng⁃Longmaxi formations shale in the Zigong area exhibits excellent reservoir properties, which is conducive to the enrichment and accumulation of deep shale gas

## 1 区域概况

四川盆地历经长期构造演化，形成复杂的海陆相叠合盆地，形成原因是反复的海侵海退及海陆变迁（邹晓艳等， ）。盆地边缘形成多个造山带和 个一级构造带，主要造山带有龙门山褶皱带、大娄山褶皱带等。自贡地区位于四川盆地的大相岭褶皱带，该带地质构造相对稳定。由于大相岭陡褶皱带内断裂总体发育程度较低，构造活动相对较弱，从而形成了相对稳定的地质构造环境，有利于页岩气的形成和保存（邹晓艳等，；何贵松等， ）。

自贡地区大地构造属于扬子克拉通—四川台坳—川中台拱—自贡台凹，地处四川盆地南部川南中心，构造位置处于四川盆地威远斜坡南翼，属于川西南斜坡低褶带自贡低褶构造（王国锋等，；张成林等， ）。研究区西接大相岭褶皱带，北接龙门山褶皱带，东接大娄山褶皱带。地形上，其西北部地势较高，东南部较低，地貌特征以浅丘和中丘为主，同时也存在一些深丘、低山以及河岸侵蚀堆积形成的地貌（王濡岳等，2021；王滢等，2022；杨雨等，2023）。

自贡地区构造起伏大，发育一系列南西—北东向褶皱构造带，形成了自贡、新店、廻龙场、高石坎、福集、蟠龙场等一系列向斜构造。五峰组—龙马溪组仅发育少量北西—南东向 级断层（断距 ～ ）。晚奥陶世—早志留世，研究区经历了加里东运动，在该运动的影响下，志留系遭受较强烈剥蚀，残余厚度 ～ ，说明加里东运动影响范围广、上升幅度大、持续时间长（张成林等， ；唐令等， ）。

## 5 结 论

（ ） 自贡地区页岩有机碳含量较高，储层具有较高的孔隙度和较好的渗透性，具有高石英含量、低黏土含量、高脆性矿物含量的特征，页岩脆性条件好。裂缝类型主要以张裂缝、剪切缝、层理缝为主，孔隙以有机质孔、黏土矿物晶间孔、黄铁矿晶间孔、白云石溶蚀孔以及残余粒间孔为主。

（ ） 自贡地区页岩含气性良好，通过开展相关性研究发现，影响含气量的因素主要是脆性矿物及黏土矿物含量、有机碳、孔隙度和地层压力。

（ ） 通过与泸州地区含气页岩进行对比，发现自贡地区页岩在孔隙度、成熟度、五峰组有机碳含量及五峰组脆性矿物含量方面均高于泸州地区，两个地区发育的孔隙类型相似，压力系数均大于 ；而自贡地区页岩的有机碳、龙马溪组脆性矿物含量及龙马溪组含气量略低于泸州地区。
